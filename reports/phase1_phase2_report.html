Core Thesis Finding: Published defect prediction performance (MCC ~0.40–0.50) is an artifact of circular self-scoring on noisy SZZ labels combined with temporal data leakage in random k-fold splits. When tested against verified oracle ground truth under time-aware streaming, real predictive capability drops by up to 85%.

Statistical Validation & Key Takeaways

Key Statistical Takeaways:
  • Leakage Inflation is Statistically Significant: Wilcoxon test for JITLine naive vs. chronological split yields p = 3.15 × 10-5 with large Cliff's δ = 0.601.
  • Self-Scoring Produces Massive Spurious Gains: BSZZ self-scoring inflates MCC by +0.190 (p = 6.54 × 10-8, Cliff's δ = 0.825).
  • Simpler Models are More Noise-Robust: LApredict (single feature) shows minimal sensitivity to SZZ variant noise compared to complex oversampled tree ensembles.
